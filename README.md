# Worldship Engineering

A research-driven reference design for a permanently inhabited, multigenerational solar-system worldship.

Every subsystem must survive shared ledgers for mass, power, heat, atmosphere, water, food, materials, maintenance labor, population, and reserves.

## Method

1. Study the strongest real and fictional worldship work.
2. Extract useful assumptions, equations, architectures, and warnings.
3. Compare competing designs rather than accepting the first plausible option.
4. Adapt the strongest parts to this ship's fixed mission and geometry.
5. Record contradictions and unresolved design debt.
6. Promote decisions only after cross-system reconciliation.

## Current status

Working baselines exist for:

- Volume I: mission, architecture, structure, rotation, propulsion, shielding
- Volume II: atmosphere, water, waste, and environmental control
- Volume III: food, agriculture, seed systems, and biological gas balance
- Volume IV: power generation, storage, distribution, and thermal rejection
- Volume V: industrial ecology, mining, manufacturing, and repair

The design remains provisional. Agriculture, radiator geometry and mass, maintenance throughput, semiconductor autonomy, construction sequencing, and long-duration social continuity remain major open problems.

## Repository layout

- `research/` source extraction and benchmark comparisons
- `decisions/` accepted working baselines and decision records
- `ledgers/` shared quantitative budgets
- `design-debt/` unresolved contradictions and verification work
- `volumes/` subsystem specifications
