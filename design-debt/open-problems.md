# Open Design Problems

These are unresolved engineering obligations. They are not decorative future work.

## Critical

1. **Agricultural yield verification**
   - Run cultivar-level crop cycles, lighting, nutrient, disease, labor, and failure simulations.
   - Verify the current 700,000 m² normal canopy can feed 2,500 people per habitat with the claimed calorie and fat margins.

2. **Radiator closure**
   - Calculate panel mass, spacing, view factors, solar orientation, plume clearance, shielding shadows, piping mass, coolant inventory, deployment, repair, and puncture tolerance.
   - Reconcile approximately 1.24 GW from both habitats plus stationary-spine and propulsion heat.

3. **Maintenance throughput**
   - Inventory component families and counts.
   - Assign failure distributions, inspection coverage, repair depth, technician time, robotic capacity, queues, spares, and common-cause failures.

4. **Construction and commissioning**
   - Explain how approximately 40.5 million tonnes are mined, fabricated, assembled, balanced, spun up, pressure-tested, biologically commissioned, and populated.

5. **Industrial autonomy**
   - Close semiconductor tooling, specialty chemicals, catalysts, precision optics, metrology, lubricants, seals, magnetic bearings, reactor parts, and mining-fleet replacement.

6. **Population and genetics**
   - Couple demographic simulation to radiation exposure, fertility, mortality, kinship, cryogenic genetic material, medical capacity, and social policy.

## Major

7. Establish the spine's full electrical-generation and thermal budgets.
8. Quantify emergency-load tiers and black-start endurance.
9. Add radiator and industrial reserves to the ship mass ledger.
10. Model biological-loop instability and mechanical bypass authority.
11. Define five-channel knowledge preservation and skill recertification.
12. Model institutional decay, capture, conflict, legitimacy, and peaceful policy replacement.
13. Define minimum viable mining fleet, replacement rate, and no-acquisition survival curves.
14. Reconcile shielding degradation with medicine and demographic outcomes.
15. Define adaptation rights for descendants, including mission revision and habitat redesign.

## Decision discipline

A problem leaves this list only when the repository contains:

- explicit assumptions;
- a calculation or simulation;
- failure cases;
- ledger updates;
- a decision record explaining why the selected architecture beat alternatives.
