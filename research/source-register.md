# Worldship Source Register

This register records ideas to test, not authorities to obey.

| Source | Strongest contribution | Warning or limitation | Current ship consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| NASA, *Space Settlements: A Design Study* | Whole-settlement systems design, construction economy, shielding, rotation, agriculture, urban layout | Based on a near-Earth settlement with external supply and a different mission | Add formal construction sequence, settlement circulation, commissioning, expansion, and mass-accounting methods |
| ESA MELiSSA | Separately controlled biological compartments treated as engineered unit processes | High closure remains experimentally difficult; biology cannot be the sole survival authority | Split waste degradation, nitrification, crop production, algae, and water polishing into bypassable compartments |
| HERITAGE | Agent-based demographic, kinship, reproductive, and genetic modeling | Results depend strongly on behavioral and radiation assumptions | Require stochastic population simulation, genetic archive, kinship controls, and periodic policy revision |
| Project Hyperion 2025 | Integrates architecture, engineering, governance, psychology, culture, and knowledge transfer | Many entries contain unresolved engineering or speculative technology | Import modularity, civic adaptability, mission-phase planning, pre-mission training society, and redundant knowledge transmission |
| Project Hyperion: Chrysalis | Construction planning, modular shell, pre-mission preparation, social belonging | Scale and propulsion assumptions do not map directly to this solar-system ship | Add decade-scale commissioning and a full population rehearsal before permanent loading |
| Project Hyperion: Helios Ark | Rare integration of propulsion, life support, governance, psychology, and mission phases | Rotation interfaces and protection geometry remain partly unresolved | Couple hardware states to institutional and psychological operating modes |
| Project Hyperion: STASS | Five-channel knowledge transmission and counter-rotating momentum management | Shielding and some structural choices are weaker | Preserve knowledge through digital, print, oral, practical, and institutional channels |
| Project Hyperion: WFP Extreme | Designs for adaptation by descendants rather than freezing founder culture | Lower technical specificity | Require reconfigurable civic space, housing, education, and governance mechanisms |
| Worldship maintenance studies | Maintenance throughput may dominate feasibility at enormous component counts | Broad estimates require ship-specific component inventories | Build component registry, failure distributions, inspection coverage, repair queues, labor model, and spares simulation |
| Atomic Rockets | Strong thermal, propulsion, shielding, and operational sanity checks | Reference encyclopedia rather than one integrated design | Use as an adversarial checklist, especially for radiator geometry, view factors, orientation, and combat/debris consequences |
| Kim Stanley Robinson, *Aurora* | Ecological drift, maintenance exhaustion, inherited consent, institutional brittleness | Fictional narrative rather than a quantitative design | Model microbial evolution, declining margins, intergenerational legitimacy, and mission exit options |
| Project Daedalus / Icarus | Formal subsystem decomposition, staged research modules, option down-selection | Primarily propulsion-focused and not a complete inhabited society | Require competing architectures, rejection records, interface definitions, and configuration control |

## Extraction rule

For every source, record:

1. the exact claim or method;
2. its assumptions;
3. whether evidence is experimental, modeled, proposed, or fictional;
4. the subsystem ledgers it changes;
5. the verification required before adoption.
